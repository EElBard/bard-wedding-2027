Our Story

⚜ ✦ ⚜

It all started in a library. Ethan was bored at work and decided to go meet the new Scheduling Specialist at the East Las Vegas Library. When he got there, he laid eyes on the most beautiful maiden in all the land. They simply couldn't stop talking, even after they both clocked out. Once getting to their respective cars, they continued to text without ever setting foot to gas pedal. Holly eventually decided that enough was enough and broke into Ethan's car so they could keep talking in person. Hours later they eventually decided to part ways, but not without a certainty that they would be seeing each other again very soon.

⚜ ✦ ⚜

The two have continued yapping every day hence forth, and don't plan on stopping any time soon.

Dress Code & Attire

👑 ✦ 👑

🌸 Encouraged: Renaissance or medieval-inspired clothing

  • Corsets, flowing skirts & peasant blouses

  • Floral crowns, circlets & fairy wings
  • Elvish or woodland fantasy inspired looks
  • Any era of "fairy tale" formal wear

🌿 Suitable: Cocktail or semi-formal attire with a spring flaire

  • A pop of color — this is a celebration!
  • Modern formal if costume feels like too much
  • Creative accessories to add a faire touch

Costumes are warmly encouraged but never required. Wear what makes you feel your most magical self! 🌿
